# Moussa Backups Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Moussa Backups is the backup feature of Moussa Rent A Car, a private Windows desktop application used by one car rental business.

## Information the app uses

Business records are stored on the owner's laptop. When the owner connects Google Drive, the app creates an encrypted backup of those records and uploads it to the owner's Google Drive.

## Google user data

The app requests only the Google Drive scope drive.file. This lets it create and access only the folder and backup files that the app itself created. It cannot read or change other files in the owner's Google Drive.

The Google sign-in token is stored encrypted on the owner's laptop using Windows account protection.

## Sharing

The app does not send business records or Google user data to the developer or to any third party, other than storing the encrypted backup with Google Drive. The data is not sold and is not used for advertising or for training artificial intelligence models.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

## Keeping and deleting data

Backups stay in the owner's Google Drive until the owner deletes them. The owner can remove the app's access at any time from the Google Account permissions page at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
